Electric turbojet propulsion.

Pulsionic is developing a jet engine that replaces fuel combustion with electrically driven plasma heating of ambient air — no kerosene, no additive, no catalyst.

§ 01 · How it works

Electricity instead of fuel.

  1. Ambient air in

    The engine draws in atmospheric air, like any jet engine.

  2. Electric plasma heating

    Electricity, not fuel, heats the airflow. Plasma heating replaces combustion.

  3. Thrust

    The heated air expands and leaves the engine at high speed, producing thrust.

§ 02 · First milestone

Stable plasma ignition in ambient air, at laboratory scale.

On April 17, 2026, Pulsionic sustained a stable plasma discharge inside a laboratory-scale cavity, operating on ambient air alone — without any additive, noble gas, or chemical catalyst.

§ 03 · Why it matters

A propulsion architecture compatible with post-fossil aviation.

01

Zero fuel.

No kerosene, no hydrogen, no sustainable aviation fuel to source, certify, and transport. The working fluid is the atmosphere itself.

02

Zero additive.

Pure ambient air — no noble gas feed, no chemical catalyst, no external reactant. No industrial supply chain to build.

03

Fully electric.

Powered entirely by electricity — from batteries, fuel cells or any future electrical source, in step with the grid's decarbonization.

§ 04 · Applications

Unmanned aircraft first.

Target markets

Small unmanned aircraft first — the shortest path from bench to flight — then larger platforms. Civil and dual-use applications.

Business model

Technology licensing and propulsion module supply to engine and aircraft manufacturers. Pulsionic does not intend to build airframes.
